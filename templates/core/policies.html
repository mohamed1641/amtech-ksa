Cancellation & Refund Policy

  1. Early Cancellation: The customer may cancel the contract and receive a full refund within one week only from the contract date.
  2. Partial Cancellation: If cancellation is requested after one week and before three weeks from the contract date, a 25% administrative fee will be deducted from the paid amount.
  3. Cancellation Restriction: After three weeks from the contract date, the customer is not entitled to cancel the contract or claim any refund.

Specifications & Amendments

  1. Pre-manufacturing Modification: The customer may request changes to product specifications, such as magnification, frame, or light source, within two weeks from the contract date.
  2. Post-delivery Modification: Within 45 days from delivery, the customer may request modifications to the design, magnification, or frame without manufacturing costs. The customer shall bear shipping and customs fees fixed at SAR 1,500.

Delivery & Delay Penalties

  1. Delivery Period: Delivery shall be within 6 to 8 weeks starting from the date of signing the contract and receiving the payment or transfer confirmation from the customer.
  2. Delay Penalty: If delivery exceeds 10 weeks, the company shall apply a 3% discount from the total contract value for each additional week of delay.

Warranty & Exclusions

  1. Warranty Scope: A one-year warranty is provided for the loupes frame and the light source, starting from the date of delivery.
  2. Warranty Exclusions: The warranty does not cover misuse, failure to follow the instruction guide, or any components manufactured from glass material.
